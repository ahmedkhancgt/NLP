# Chapter 12: Use Cases in Modern Era: Introduction

## Core Idea
AI's market-wide growth (projected $1.6 trillion by 2030, from $119.78 billion in 2022) is disproportionately driven by NLP ($22% of the AI market, ~39% CAGR), and ChatGPT's explosive user growth is the single most visible proof point of that shift — this chapter is the transition point from "technical GPT history" (Ch 1-11) into the book's industry-by-industry use-case survey (Ch 13-26).

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Market-sizing lens for AI opportunity assessment**: triangulate total AI market size, the NLP sub-segment's share and growth rate, and a specific product's (ChatGPT's) revenue trajectory to justify investment urgency.
  - When to use: building a business case for AI/NLP adoption or investment.
  - How: cite the macro AI market CAGR, narrow to the NLP sub-market CAGR (typically higher), then point to a flagship product's growth curve as the proof-of-concept accelerant.

## Key Concepts
- **Global AI market**: $119.78B (end of 2022) projected to $1,600B by 2030.
- **NLP market share**: ~22% of the total AI market, with ~39% CAGR to 2030; chatbot segment specifically at ~30.29% CAGR (2022-2027).
- **Asia Pacific AI CAGR**: ~42% (2022-2030), outpacing the global average.
- **ChatGPT revenue projection**: ~$200M (2023) scaling toward ~$1B (2024), reaching 1→100 million users in roughly 3 months.

## Mental Models
- Use the **"core vs. non-core sector AI adoption" split** (software/IT ~41% market share, media/advertising ~22%) to calibrate which industries are AI-native adopters versus AI-adjacent adopters.
- Treat **ChatGPT's user-growth speed itself as market evidence** — the chapter uses the product's own adoption curve as the leading indicator that justifies the macro market projections, a rhetorical pattern worth recognizing (and scrutinizing) when reading AI market forecasts generally.

## Key Takeaways
1. NLP, not AI broadly, is the specific growth engine behind the current AI investment wave, per this book's framing.
2. ChatGPT's user-growth speed (1→100M users in ~3 months) functions as the headline case study the rest of the book's industry chapters implicitly reference.
3. This chapter is explicitly transitional — it exists to justify why the book spends 14+ subsequent chapters on industry-specific use cases.
4. Net revenue projections ($200M→$1B) framed ChatGPT, at time of writing, as a nascent but fast-scaling commercial product rather than only a research demo.
