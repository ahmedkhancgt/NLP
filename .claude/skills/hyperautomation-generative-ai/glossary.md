# Glossary

**Accountability (AI principle)** — Ensuring clear lines of responsibility for AI system actions and outcomes. (Ch7)

**AI Center (formerly AI Fabric)** — UiPath's platform for deploying, managing, and continually improving ML models inside RPA workflows. (Ch3)

**Algorithmic bias** — Discriminatory outcomes caused by biased training data (e.g., facial recognition less accurate on darker skin tones). (Ch7)

**Anti-Money Laundering (AML)** — Regulatory process for detecting/preventing money laundering, commonly automated via RPA/AI pattern detection. (Ch1, Ch4, Ch5, Ch10)

**Application Modeller** — Blue Prism component exposing a target application's UI elements for automation. (Ch3)

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** — Branch of computer science enabling machines to mimic human intelligence (learning, planning, reasoning, decision-making). (Ch1, Ch6, Ch7)

**Artificial Neural Network (ANN)** — Feed-forward network of layered perceptrons/neurons modeling relationships in data. (Ch6, Ch8)

**Augmenting robots** — Robots (e.g., exoskeletons, prosthetics) designed to enhance human physical capability. (Ch2)

**Autonomous robots** — Robots operating independently of human control via sensors (e.g., Roomba). (Ch2)

**Backpropagation** — Neural network training technique adjusting weights based on prior epoch's error rate. (Ch8)

**Big Data** — Technology for storing, analyzing, and managing large data volumes to recognize patterns; a key unit of Hyperautomation. (Ch4)

**Blue Prism** — Enterprise-grade RPA tool (founded 2001) with four components: Object Studio, Process Studio, Application Modeller, Control Room. (Ch3)

**Center of Excellence (CoE)** — Centralized governance body standardizing and overseeing automation scaling across an organization. (Ch5, Ch9)

**ChatOps** — Using chat-based collaboration tools for real-time coordination during incident management. (Ch10)

**Chatbot** — Software built on ML/AI/NLP that can hold conversational interactions with humans via text or speech. (Ch2, Ch4, Ch6)

**Cobot (collaborative robot)** — Robot that learns multiple tasks to assist humans; compact, flexible, easy to install. (Ch2, Ch4)

**Cognitive Automation** — Automation adding "thinking" (pattern recognition, decision-making on unstructured data) to RPA's "doing." (Ch2, Ch4)

**Computer vision** — Technology enabling machines to understand images/video by breaking them into analyzable parts. (Ch4, Ch6)

**Configurable AI** — AI designed to be modified/adapted to specific user needs and contexts. (Ch7)

**Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD)** — Software/model delivery practice of frequent, automated testing and deployment. (Ch5, Ch8)

**Control Room** — Blue Prism's interface for scheduling, monitoring, and administering automation processes and runtime resources. (Ch3)

**Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)** — Neural network type specialized for image/video data using convolutional and pooling layers. (Ch8)

**DALL-E** — Multimodal generative AI model producing images from natural-language text input. (Ch11)

**Deep Learning** — ML subset using multi-layer artificial neural networks to classify, infer, and predict. (Ch1, Ch6, Ch8)

**Defense in Depth** — Blue Prism's layered security approach (detect, monitor, block, investigate). (Ch3)

**Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Model (DDPM)** — Generative model type using forward/reverse noise diffusion to generate high-quality content. (Ch11)

**Dependent robots** — Non-autonomous robots that interact with and enhance human actions. (Ch2)

**Digital Twin Organization (DTO)** — A virtual representation of a product/organization enabled by RPA + other platforms. (Ch1, Ch4, Ch5)

**Explainable AI** — AI providing clear, understandable reasoning for its decisions. (Ch7)

**Fixed/Hard Automation** — Automation with an unchanging process sequence; high volume, low flexibility, low per-unit cost. (Ch2)

**Flexible/Soft Automation** — Automation producing varied products with no time lost reprogramming; medium volume. (Ch2)

**Flexible Machining Systems** — A technology category within Integrated Automation. (Ch2)

**Fraud detection and prevention** — A recurring Hyperautomation/Generative AI use case across banking, insurance, and contact centers. (Ch4, Ch9, Ch10, Ch11)

**GAN (Generative Adversarial Network)** — Generative model (2014) with a generator and discriminator competing iteratively; fast, high-quality, less diverse. (Ch11)

**Generative AI** — AI that produces new content (text, images, audio, synthetic data) rather than only classifying/predicting. (Ch11)

**Gradient descent** — Optimization algorithm minimizing the difference between actual and predicted output. (Ch8)

**Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)** — Research domain studying safe, collaborative coexistence of humans and robots sharing workspaces. (Ch2)

**Humanoid robots** — Robots designed to mimic human appearance/behavior (e.g., Sophia, Atlas). (Ch2)

**Hyperautomation** — Gartner's term for a business-driven, disciplined approach to rapidly identify, vet, and automate as many business/IT processes as possible, combining RPA, AI, ML, and other technologies. (Ch1–Ch11)

**iBPM / iBPMS (Intelligent Business Process Management [Suites])** — Platforms combining process automation, monitoring, and optimization with AI/ML for intelligent workflows. (Ch4, Ch5, Ch9, Ch10)

**Independent robots** — Robots working fully without human intervention (e.g., bomb disposal). (Ch2)

**Integration Platform as a Service (iPaaS)** — Cloud-based platform enabling integration across multiple applications/data sources within Hyperautomation. (Ch4, Ch5)

**Intelligent Automation (IA)** — RPA combined with AI/ML/NLP/computer vision/BPM for end-to-end automation with decision-making capability; distinct from Hyperautomation. (Ch4)

**Intelligent Process Automation (IPA)** — Logic-driven automation using pre-programmed rules, primarily on structured data. (Ch2)

**IQ Bot** — Automation Anywhere's cognitive automation feature extracting data from unstructured/semi-structured documents using OCR, NLP, computer vision, and ML. (Ch3)

**Know Your Customer (KYC)** — Customer identity verification process commonly automated in banking via RPA/Hyperautomation. (Ch1, Ch3, Ch4, Ch5, Ch9, Ch10)

**LaMDA** — Transformer-based language model trained specifically on dialogue data. (Ch11)

**Large Language Model (LLM)** — Generative AI model with billions/trillions of parameters capable of generating text, code, and more. (Ch11)

**LLaMA** — Compact, efficient autoregressive language model trained on a larger number of tokens than typical for its parameter count. (Ch11)

**Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)** — RNN variant solving the vanishing gradient problem via input/output/forget gates and memory cells. (Ch8)

**Machine Learning (ML)** — AI subfield using algorithms/data to learn and make predictions/decisions without explicit programming. (Ch1, Ch4, Ch6, Ch8)

**ML Package** — UiPath AI Center artifact containing metadata/code needed to train and serve an ML model. (Ch3)

**MLOps (Machine Learning Operations)** — DevOps-inspired practice of running ML/DL models through a repeatable, automated workflow to deploy them to production. (Ch8)

**ModelOps** — Enterprise-level governance and lifecycle-management framework for deployed ML models at scale. (Ch8)

**Model drift** — Degradation of a deployed model's performance/relevance over time. (Ch8)

**Natural Language Processing (NLP)** — Technology enabling machines to read, understand, and interpret human language. (Ch1, Ch2, Ch4, Ch6)

**Nonpublic Personal Information (NPI)** — Sensitive personal data (e.g., SSNs) that must be redacted for privacy compliance. (Ch5)

**Object Studio** — Blue Prism component for creating reusable Visual Business Objects (VBOs). (Ch3)

**Optical Character Recognition (OCR)** — Technology extracting text from images/scanned documents. (Ch3, Ch5, Ch11)

**Orchestrator** — UiPath's centralized web-based tool for deploying, monitoring, and managing automated bots/processes. (Ch3)

**Perceptron** — Simple linear binary classifier; the basic unit of a neural network. (Ch8)

**Pre-programmed robots** — Robots operating in controlled environments on repetitive, fixed tasks (e.g., assembly-line arms). (Ch2)

**Process Design Document (PDD)** — Documentation of a business process's flow, steps, discrepancies, and time estimates, used before automating. (Ch4)

**Process Studio** — Blue Prism component for designing/editing/testing business processes built from VBOs. (Ch3)

**Process mining** — Analyzing event logs/data to identify process inefficiencies, bottlenecks, and automation opportunities. (Ch4, Ch9, Ch10)

**ProGen** — Multimodal generative model trained on protein samples to generate proteins from natural-language descriptions. (Ch11)

**Prompt-tuning / Adaptors** — Techniques to customize large generative models for proprietary data without full fine-tuning. (Ch11)

**Reactive AI** — AI reacting to current input via predefined rules with no learning/prediction capability. (Ch6)

**Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)** — Training method aligning generative model outputs with human-rated preferences. (Ch11)

**Responsible AI** — AI designed and used to be transparent, explainable, and configurable, considering societal/environmental impact. (Ch7)

**Robotic Process Automation (RPA)** — Software technology automating rule-based, repetitive tasks via bots using pre-programmed rules on structured data. (Ch1–Ch5, Ch9, Ch10)

**Self-Aware AI** — The most advanced, hypothetical AI type exhibiting human-like consciousness and mental states. (Ch6)

**Stable Diffusion** — Text-to-image generative model using iterative noise reduction to align output with text description. (Ch11)

**Teleoperated robots** — Semi-autonomous robots controlled remotely by humans via wireless networks. (Ch2)

**Tesseract OCR** — OCR engine (v3.05.01) used in Blue Prism V6 for complex text recognition. (Ch3)

**Theory of Mind AI** — AI type capable of understanding/adjusting to human emotions in interaction; largely aspirational. (Ch6)

**Transformer (architecture)** — Neural network architecture (2017) using self-attention and positional encoding, foundational to most modern LLMs. (Ch11)

**Transparent AI** — AI whose decision-making processes are visible and understandable to users. (Ch7)

**Trustworthy AI (4 pillars)** — Safety, Reliability, Transparency, Accountability as the measurable basis for public/user trust in AI. (Ch7)

**Variational Autoencoder (VAE)** — Encoder-decoder generative model enabling fast sampling of new data representations; lower detail than diffusion models. (Ch11)

**Visual Business Object (VBO)** — Blue Prism's reusable automation interface to a single external application. (Ch3)

**Work queues** — Blue Prism's mechanism for dynamically controlling the number of robots working a given task queue. (Ch3)

**Zero-shot / Few-shot learning** — A generative model's ability to perform tasks with no or minimal task-specific training examples. (Ch11)
